How picks are chosen

PLAY — college: the fitted totals model at ≥58% (tested 62.9 / 65.2 / 55.4% on seasons it never saw), or the underdog implied to score ≤10 → OVER (67% this season). Skipped when another signal points the opposite way on the same total, or when an OVER would be played into 20+ mph gusts.

PLAY — NFL: only when two measured signals agree: "both teams' last game went under → OVER" (55 / 59 / 63% the last three seasons) and the model on the same side at ≥55%. The NFL board is break-even this season, so NFL plays are rarer and stay at $10.

LEAN: model 55–58% in college, or ≥58% alone in the NFL. $5, or skip.
