How to read this
Each card leads with the call: bet this side, this much, or no bet. A call only appears when a play is STRONG — hard evidence (a QB ruled out, real line movement, a live pace gap, 25+ mph gusts backed by a second signal) or several weighted signals agreeing. Leans are shown as chips so you can see what's brewing, but they are never a bet. Stakes are quarter-Kelly on your bankroll. Reference only — nothing here places a bet, and never bet what you can't afford to lose.
