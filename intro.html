The part most tools skip
It grades its own calls

Every strong call is logged and graded when the game ends — wins and losses. If a call fades before kickoff, it's marked and kept out of the record, so nothing gets flattered after the fact.

One honest caveat
It won't beat the book for you

Sportsbooks price weather and injuries fast. This surfaces where the line and the conditions disagree, with the reasoning shown — so you decide. Bet only what you'd shrug off losing.
